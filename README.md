## What it is

[BlinkPop](https://blinkpop.video/?utm_source=github&utm_medium=promo) is an AI video effects site. Upload a photo, pick a trend, and get a 15-second vertical clip ready for TikTok, Reels or Shorts in a few minutes. No editing and no prompt writing.

AI video trends move fast. By the time most people find the right app, template and prompt, the trend is over. BlinkPop puts each new trend on its own page with the effect already tuned, so you only bring the photo.

## Who it's for

- **TikTok and Reels creators** who want to join a trend while it is still hot.
- **Pet owners.** Most effects also work with a cat or dog photo ([AI cat videos](https://blinkpop.video/ai-cat-video), [AI dog videos](https://blinkpop.video/ai-dog-video)).
- **Anyone who wants a fun clip of themselves, a partner or a friend** without learning a video editor.

## Features

- **One photo in, one video out.** No prompts, no timeline, no editing.
- **Works with pets** on most effects.
- **AI audio or silent.** Go silent to add the original trending sound on TikTok.
- **Phone friendly.** Upload from your phone and send the finished video back to it with a QR code.
- **Fair pricing.** Free credits for your first video, then one-time credit packs with no subscription. Failed videos are refunded automatically. See [pricing](https://blinkpop.video/pricing).
- **Want something longer or fully custom?** Order a [custom video](https://blinkpop.video/custom-video).
- Available in English, Español, Português and 日本語.
